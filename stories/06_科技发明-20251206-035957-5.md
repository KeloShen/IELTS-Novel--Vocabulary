# The Unlikely Polytechnic of Elon Musk & Taylor Swift

Bob was **meticulous**, checking every **gauge** twice. He believed his calculations were **accurate**. "The **hydraulic** **pump** should **compress** the waste at precisely the right **phase**," he muttered, **adjusting** a **valve**. He failed to **concentrate** on the **ventilation** **system**, a critical **auxiliary** component. This **flaw** was a **contingency** he hadn't planned for.

She pressed play, but the **tape** snarled. It was a simple **mistake**—she had inserted it **wrong**. The **appliance** whirred pathetically. Meanwhile, Elon was in the corner, attempting to **assemble** a **microcomputer** from **binary** code he'd scribbled on a napkin. He **stumbled** over a cable, causing his **prototype** to **shift** and **alter** its **mode** from "compute" to "lightly toast bread."

Bob tried to **measure** the pressure. "I must **calculate** this again," he said, **focusing** his **optics** through a **microscope** **lens** onto a tiny **sensor**. He needed to **pinpoint** the exact **aspect** of the **process** that was failing. The **byproduct** of his current test was a smell so potent it seemed to have its own **gravity**. This **circumstance** was not in the manual. The **tool** he was using to **filter** the output was a kitchen sieve, an **accessory** wholly unsuited to the task. He hadn't even begun to **distil** the final product. The **framework** of his plan was crumbling. He realized he needed to completely **refine** his **technique**. Perhaps he should **simplify** his approach. But first, he had to **operate** the emergency **drainage** **pump** before the lab **condensed** into a swamp of failure.

## Chapter 2: The Network of Nonsense

The **technology** in the polytechnic was a **patchwork** of eras. A **wireless** **keyboard** from 2005 sat next to a gleaming new **laptop**. A massive **radar** dish, meant to **detect** incoming pizza deliveries, was hooked up to a **loudspeaker** from a 1970s **stereo** system. The entire **network** was a **mess**. She **discovered** that by **turning** the **screen** brightness down, the kitten looked profoundly existential. "This is a **breakthrough** in feline emotional **analysis**!" she exclaimed. Bob, the engineer, was now **evaluating** the sewage-perfume disaster. He needed to **assess** the damage. He began to **gather** data, using an **instrument** to **count** the number of foul-smelling molecules per cubic foot. His **estimate** was "too many." To **correct** it, he would have to **adapt** the entire **assembly**. New **equipment** was needed. He submitted a request for a **hydraulic** **mould** to create a better **valve**.

"**Focus**!" yelled Elon, who was now trying to **facilitate** his sigh-generator by having Taylor read her old diaries into a **microphone**. A **tester** from a **rival** company visited, took one look at the **facility**, and laughed so hard he **triggered** the **sensor** on the automatic sprinklers. Every **circuit** and **wire** seemed to **belong** to a different, incompatible **science fiction** novel.
