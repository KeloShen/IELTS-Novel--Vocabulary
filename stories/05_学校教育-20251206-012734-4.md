**The Unlikely Ascension of Gary the Intern**

**Chapter 1: The Inauspicious Beginning**

"You are an **anathema** to productivity!" she declared, her anger a **seething**, barely-contained force. She marched him to the roof for a "motivational talk," which involved a lot of pointing towards the edge. "Your performance is **reprehensible**," she hissed. "It **besmirches** the very name of this company!"

But fate, in its absurd humor, intervened. A sudden gust, a misplaced step, and Brenda’s **strident** lecture was cut short as she stumbled backwards over a low vent. Gary, moving with an **unwonted** burst of speed (prompted more by panic than heroism), lunged and grabbed her wrist as she dangled over the precipice. In that moment of suspended terror, a **lucrative** idea, clear and sharp, pierced the fog of Gary’s mind. He looked down at Brenda’s terrified face. "My continued silence," he said, his voice surprisingly steady, "has a price."

**Chapter 2: The Art of the Shakedown**
