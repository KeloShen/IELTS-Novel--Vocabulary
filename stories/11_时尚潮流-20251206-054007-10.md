# The Jade Gown of Absurdity

## Chapter 1: The Vogue That Prevailed

In the glittering, absurd world of high **fashion**, one **trend** reigned supreme: wearing live snails as **jewelry**. This bizarre **current** of **popularity** had begun as a joke but now was utterly in **vogue**. The **tendency** to accessorize with mollusks **prevailed** on every red carpet. The **icon** of this movement was Elon Musk, who had inexplicably become a **model** and **idol** for the slimy **style**. He claimed it was a protest against **extravagant luxury**, though his **gem**-encrusted snail shells suggested otherwise.

His rival in influence was Taylor Swift, who refused the snail **vogue**. Instead, she preferred to **adorn** herself with **exquisite jade** and rare **gems**. She believed true **elegance** couldn’t be achieved with something that left a **stain**. Her **appearance** was always **perfect**, a **grace** of **decent** tailoring and subtle **cosmetics**. She found the public’s **fascination** with snails **ugly** and lacking in **charm**. “A **beautiful garment**,” she’d say, adjusting the **sleeve** of her silk **gown**, “should not come with its own **slime**.”

Meanwhile, in a **shabby** workshop, a **tailor** named Bob **spun** a conspiracy. He believed the snail **trend** was a **masquerade**, a **veil** for a deeper plot. He worked day and night, his fingers **stitching** with a **meticulous** focus, creating a single **garment** from a **textile woven** with **velvet** and **wool**. He used no **dye** or **pigment**, letting the natural **colour**—a soft **grey**—**prevail**. His goal was not to follow the **style**, but to destroy it.

## Chapter 2: The Costume of Chaos

The day of the Grand Gala arrived. The theme was “Garden,” and guests took it literally. Elon Musk arrived in an **overall** made of grass, a **cloak** of leaves, and a **hat** with a wide **brim** hosting a family of snails. A **bracelet** of pearls (and snails) **clasped** his wrist. He looked **ridiculous**, but the crowd was **fascinated**. Taylor Swift wore a **slender** gown the **color** of **jade**, its **collar** high and **elegant**. A simple **necklace** with a single **bead** of amber **adorned** her throat. She was **pretty** and **charming**, a vision of classic **beauty**.

Bob the tailor, however, had not been invited. He **clothed** himself in his creation—a dull **grey robe**—and slipped in. Under his arm, he carried a **purse** made of **patches** and **rags**. Inside was his weapon: not a **gun**, but a **handkerchief** soaked in salt.

The gala was a spectacle of **embellishment**. Gowns were **embroidered** with real flowers, **jackets** had **stripes** of **velvet**, and **scarves** were **woven** with **gold thread**. **Jewellery** glittered everywhere, from **elaborate** headpieces to **bracelets** that **clasped** upper arms. The air was thick with perfume and the **pigment** of **make-up**. Bob moved through the crowd, his **slight figure** unnoticed. He saw a **model** adjusting a **strap** on her **slipper**, a **handsome** actor fixing the **button** on his **vest**, and a socialite whose **dyed**-**pink** hair was an **extravagant** feat of **hairdressing**.

Then, he saw his target: Elon Musk’s prized snail, Sir Slime, perched on his **shoulder** like a **jewel**. Bob’s plan was **simple**. He would expose the **trend** as fragile. As he reached for his salty **handkerchief**, Taylor Swift, sensing chaos, gracefully stepped on the **lace** of his **boot**. Bob stumbled, and the **purse** flew from his hand, spilling not the **handkerchief**, but hundreds of tiny, **faded brown buttons**.

## Chapter 3: The Unraveling Thread

The **buttons** scattered like **beads**, causing a chain reaction. A **model** in **tight jeans** slipped on one, grabbing a **tablecloth** for support, which pulled down a centerpiece of **dyed purple** orchids. The **stain** of water and **pigment** splashed across Elon’s grass **overalls**, causing the snails to retreat into their shells in protest. The **vogue** for live accessories began to **fade** right there on the spot.

“My **costume**!” Elon cried, looking **truly ugly** in his **stained**, wilted **garments**. The **elegance** of the evening was **blotted** out by absurdity. Taylor Swift shook her head, the **ribbon** in her hair fluttering. “**Fashion** should **embellish** the person, not **enslave** them to a **ridiculous trend**,” she declared, her voice full of **grace**.

In the commotion, Bob’s **grey robe** got caught on a **chain** decorating the staircase. With a **rip**, a **sleeve** tore, revealing the **exquisite** lining he had **sewn** inside—a reverse of the **robe** made from **scarlet silk**. The crowd gasped. It was **beautiful**. The **shabby exterior** had been a **veil**, a **masquerade**. He wasn’t a poor tailor, but a **artist** protesting the **shallow** nature of **vogue**.

Elon Musk, his **popularity** waning as quickly as the **color** from his **dyed** grass suit, looked from his **stained overalls** to Bob’s revealed **masterpiece**. “What is the **meaning** of this?” he demanded.

Bob straightened the **collar** of his torn **robe**. “You used **luxury** as an **ornament** for **foolishness**. I used a **rag** to **hide artistry**. Which **style** has more **value**?” He pointed to the **stained**, **faded** mess of the snail **trend**. “Your **jewellery** leaves a **trail**. My **stitches** hold a **story**.”

From her **purse**, Taylor Swift pulled a pair of **fabric shears**. With a **decent** and **practiced** snip, she cut the **string** of pearls (and snails) from Elon’s wrist. The snails were freed. Then, she walked to Bob and, in a gesture of pure **charm**, **pinned** a single, **slender jade** **bead** to his **robe**. “A **gem** for the true **icon**,” she said.

The **prevailing tendency** in the room shifted. People looked at their **extravagant**, **stained costumes** with **disgust**. They eyed their **tight**, **uncomfortable garments**. The **vogue** was over. As the crowd departed, leaving a **laundry** list of ruined **clothes**—**overcoats**, **sweaters**, **skirts**, **trousers**, **stockings**, and one **velvet cloak**—a new **current** began. The **trend** for the next season, sparked by Bob’s **reveal** and Taylor’s **approval**, was for **comfort**, for **textiles** that felt good, for **colors** that didn’t **fade**, and for **jewellery** that didn’t **escape**.

Bob returned to his workshop, not to **sew** **garments** for others, but to **spin** his own **thread**. He finally took off the **grey robe**, now **adorned** with a **jade** **bead**, and hung it in his **wardrobe**. He put on a simple **white sweater** and **brown trousers**. He looked **plain**, but he felt **perfect**. Outside, the moon was the **color** of **pale yellow**, and for the first time, he didn’t think about **fashion** at all. He just thought it was **beautiful**.
