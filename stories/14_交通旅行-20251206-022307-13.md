**The Unfortunate Voyage of the SS Swift Conveyance**

The **pilot** announced he’d "mostly" passed the simulator. **Chapter 2: Highways, Haunts, and Harbour Horrors**

The **flight** was a **lull** between disasters. They **arrived** at a **port** city where the **harbour** smelled of fish and regret. His next **leg** was by sea. The **ferry**, named "The **Steamer** Rustbucket," was a **vessel** that listed **ominously**. The **captain**, a grizzled **seaman**, pointed a shaky finger at the **dock**. "All **aboard**! He sped past a broken-down **wagon**, a **pedicab** pedaling heroically uphill, and a **cart** selling suspicious meat. A **signpost** pointed him toward the desert. He turned onto a lonely **lane**, the **path** marked by **cacti** and **tumbleweeds**. The **traffic** was nonexistent, but the **hazards** were now **coyotes** and his own **dwindling** sanity. The **van**’s **tire** blew with a **bang**. "Perfect," he sighed.
