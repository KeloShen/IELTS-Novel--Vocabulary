**The Unfortunate Voyage of Captain Elon’s Flying Safari**

The first **hazard** was paperwork. Elon’s **visa** for “Imaginary Airspace” was denied. “**Postpone** the launch?” he scoffed. “Never! One woman wept over her flattened **suitcase**. “Your **carry-on** is safe, madam,” the steward said, handing her a single pickle.

The **highway** to the **airfield** was **blocked**.
